# Sabi Shop --- Onboarding Specification

**Phase:** 5 --- Product Experience\
**Status:** Working Specification

## 1. Purpose

Get a small-business owner from first access to trustworthy daily
operation without requiring accounting expertise.

## 2. First-Run Sequence

1.  establish owner/business identity;
2.  configure essential business information;
3.  add/import initial products;
4.  configure users/roles;
5.  establish opening cash where applicable;
6.  explain core operating concepts;
7.  perform a guided first sale;
8.  verify the business can see the resulting record.

## 3. Teaching Model

Onboarding teaches the few concepts users must understand: - sale vs
payment; - cash vs profit; - stock movement; - customer debt; - supplier
liability; - corrections vs deletion; - offline/sync state.

## 4. Progressive Disclosure

Do not expose the full system at first launch. Introduce advanced
controls when the business reaches them.

## 5. Business Safety

Initial setup should make authorization boundaries and historical-record
behavior clear.

## 6. Recovery

An interrupted onboarding process must be resumable without creating
duplicate business records.

## 7. Completion

Onboarding is complete when the owner/manager can perform a normal sale,
locate it, understand its payment/stock effect and understand where
management controls live.

# Sabi Shop --- MVP / V1 Scope & V2 Backlog

**Phase:** 7 --- Prove It Works\
**Status:** FINAL — RECONCILED V1

## 1. V1 Objective

Prove that Sabi Shop can serve a real small retail business as a
trustworthy daily operating system.

## 2. V1 Must Have

-   business/user/role management;
-   product catalogue and search;
-   sales/POS;
-   payments;
-   customer credit/debt;
-   repayments;
-   supplier/purchasing;
-   inventory;
-   pricing/discount controls;
-   returns;
-   corrections;
-   cash movement/reconciliation;
-   staff activity;
-   business performance;
-   receipts;
-   offline operation;
-   synchronization;
-   audit/integrity;
-   permissions;
-   essential notifications;
-   English/Pidgin content foundation.


## 2A. V1 Business-Integrity Commitments

The V1 scope includes the resolved business-integrity behaviors, not merely the feature labels: weighted-average costing; visible negative-stock exceptions; tax/VAT; operational business-day sessions across midnight; configurable cash custody; reconciliation using Expected Cash versus physical Actual Cash; configurable 15-minute-default corrections; Owner visibility for consequential Manager self-corrections; verified/logged transfer confirmation; classified configurable payment methods; supplier-return settlement semantics; and dual-gate salesperson incentives.

## 3. V1 Quality Bar

V1 is not "feature complete" merely because screens exist. It must
reliably preserve money, stock, debt and historical truth.

## 4. V2 Candidates

Candidates already identified include: - barcode scanning; -
branch/multi-location management; - advanced CRM; - advanced
accounting; - richer analytics; - broader integrations; - dedicated
SMS/printing infrastructure; - stronger external integrity anchoring; -
more sophisticated enterprise functionality.

## 5. Scope Rule

A V2 feature must not be allowed to weaken V1 reliability.

## 6. Release Gate

V1 requires successful acceptance, QA, scenario and failure testing plus
operational readiness.

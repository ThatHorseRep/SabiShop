# Sabi Shop --- Language & Content Specification

**Phase:** 5 --- Product Experience\
**Status:** Working Specification

## 1. Purpose

Define product language for English and Nigerian Pidgin without treating
Pidgin as mechanical word-for-word translation.

## 2. Voice

Sabi Shop should sound: - clear; - practical; - respectful; - calm; -
locally understandable; - confident without sounding corporate.

## 3. Content Principle

Use the simplest wording that accurately communicates the business
state.

## 4. Language Modes

English and Nigerian Pidgin should share the same semantic message keys.
Translation changes wording, not business behavior.

## 5. Consequential Messages

Confirmation text must state the real consequence. Avoid vague labels
such as "Done" where the action affects debt, inventory, cash or
historical records.

## 6. Error Messages

Every error should answer: - what happened; - whether anything was
saved; - what to do next.

## 7. Financial Language

Do not use "profit", "cash", "sales", "debt", "balance" or similar terms
interchangeably. Labels must reflect the underlying business concept.

## 8. Pidgin Quality

Pidgin copy should feel natural to Nigerian users and should be reviewed
by native/experienced speakers. Do not manufacture slang merely to
appear local.

## 9. Content Governance

Final production copy is controlled content. Copy changes must not alter
business rules.
